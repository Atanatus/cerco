Expedition rules and abilities

Expedition rules and abilities

Fight through the horde, stack upgrades and reach a sanctuary every five rooms. Health pickups are scattered on the floor. You have 45 seconds to recover, gear up and move on.

◉ Singularity · ϟ Chain Lightning · ▣ Turrets · ✹ Detonators · ◌ Phantoms · ✚ Healers · ◎ Homing Hunters · ☢ Contamination · ⬡ Shields · ◈ Merchant · ×3 Combo · ☢ Nuke

Close Quarters trades bullet travel for damage; Longshot rewards traveled distance (including homing paths). They cannot be combined. Shatter Sprint, Corrosive Wake, Sprint Drive, and Momentum Recovery work only while actually sprinting. Demolishers attack turrets before players when visible. Orbit Magnet gathers nearby conventional drops, but not Nukes or score beacons. Piercing Force rewards successive hits by the same projectile. Sprint Mastery unlocks advanced sprint abilities. Rare score beacons grant ×1.5 kill points temporarily; they are not Style Coins.

Singularity: an optional autonomous gravity anchor; it pulls clustered small enemies along walkable tiles without damaging them. Guardian and heavy units resist it. No extra controls required.

Eight-direction movement: Q W E A S D Z C. Mouse: aim and fire. Space: sprint. On mobile: move with the left joystick, hold FIRE on the right to shoot, drag FIRE to aim, and hold SPRINT to run. Turrets: maintain one active turret, or two with Dual Deployment; lost turrets redeploy after their 7-second cooldown. Auto-fire: an optional upgrade. F: toggle Auto-Move; it stays enabled when you hit a wall.

Enemy tactics evolve with combat progress: faster projectiles, predictive interception, sharper homing with a close-range dodge window, and more dangerous guardian salvos in advanced rooms. Rooms 7, 17, 27… feature ranged-only assaults. From later rooms onward, enemy teams evolve, and prolonged advanced frenzies trigger a spreading invasion from the room edges. Guardian rooms 4, 9, 14… rotate several guardian types, including a giant Detonator Matriarch that spits out dangerous but nonrewarding bombs. Defeat every guardian and survive any marked death blast to unlock the exit. Sanctuaries appear in rooms 5, 10, 15… Your score is never spent. Gold Style Coins are collected for cosmetic rewards on ATANATUS; they are separate from shop credits and are subject to platform verification.

Hold the mouse button to fire manually, even with Auto-fire. Turrets redeploy when a slot opens and their 7-second cooldown is ready. Auto-move stays enabled when you hit a wall; choose another direction. Life Steal carries its kill counter across rooms. Base movement speed increases with progression. Shop credits, Style Coins and score are all separate. Collect gold coins for cosmetic rewards on ATANATUS; the website must verify them.
